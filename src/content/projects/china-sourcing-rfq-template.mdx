Use this outline when requesting quotations from a China supplier shortlist. Send the same controlled package to every candidate and require deviations to be stated.

## Buyer and project

- Buyer company and responsible person
- Project name and RFQ revision
- Intended destination market
- Target order date and required delivery window
- Confidentiality or intellectual-property instructions

## Product definition

- Product name, model, and current drawing revision
- Intended use and user
- Dimensions, tolerances, materials, colors, finish, and components
- Reference sample status
- Required performance and test methods
- Prohibited substitutions
- Required destination-market compliance evidence

## Quantity and commercial request

Ask for unit prices at several quantity levels. Separate tooling, setup, sample, packaging, labeling, testing, inspection, and documentation charges.

Require the quotation to state:

- MOQ for finished product, each variant, material, and custom packaging;
- sample and production lead time;
- Incoterms® 2020 rule and exact named place;
- quotation currency, validity, and payment milestones;
- quantity overrun or underrun tolerance;
- estimated carton size, gross weight, and units per carton;
- assumptions and deviations from the RFQ.

## Supplier response evidence

Request the Chinese legal company name, business-license copy, factory address, manufacturing role, relevant equipment, comparable-product experience, current capacity assumptions, outsourced processes, and proposed quality plan.

## Comparison rule

Do not normalize quotations until missing assumptions are resolved. A lower quote based on generic packaging, a different material, or excluded testing is not directly comparable.

Version the RFQ, retain supplier responses, and reference the final approved specification in the purchase order.

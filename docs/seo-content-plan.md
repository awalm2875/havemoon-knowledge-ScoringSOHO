# Havemoon SEO Content Plan

Reviewed: 2026-08-10

## Editorial rules

- Audience: overseas buyers and importers sourcing manufactured goods from China.
- Search strategy: pair a broad topic phrase with one specific buyer question; use both naturally in the title, introduction, major headings, image-free checklists, and internal anchor text.
- People-first standard: each article must leave the reader with a usable document, decision sequence, checklist, or comparison method. There is no target keyword density and no minimum word count used as a ranking proxy.
- Trust boundary: no invented transactions, factory visits, prices, lead-time benchmarks, legal conclusions, or statistics. Jurisdiction-specific compliance questions are referred to the relevant authority or qualified adviser.
- On-page structure: one descriptive title/H1, concise meta description, direct answer in the opening, ordered H2 sections, scannable tables or lists, contextual internal links, a practical conclusion, and an educational-information note when the topic touches contracts, customs, or compliance.
- Dates: `date` records the real publication date; `updatedDate` changes only after a material review.

## Internal-link architecture

1. Discovery pages link to supplier verification and sample ordering.
2. Sample ordering links to product specifications, the purchase-order checklist, and pre-production approval.
3. Purchase-order and packaging guides link to MOQ economics, production scheduling, and inspection.
4. Production guides link to factory audits, pre-shipment inspection, freight quote comparison, Incoterms, and landed cost.
5. Compliance and product-specific guides link back to the relevant specification, sample, inspection, and landed-cost pages.

Each guide should contain two to five descriptive internal links. Anchors describe the destination decision rather than using generic phrases such as “click here.”

## SEO and AdSense checks

- Titles are unique, descriptive, and concise; no title repeats a keyword variant merely to increase frequency.
- Descriptions state the guide's practical coverage and differ from the title.
- All new pages are indexable, canonicalized by the existing layout, included in the sitemap, and represented as `Article` structured data.
- The homepage, category pages, latest-guide list, activity heatmap, RSS feed, and Pagefind index update from the collection automatically.
- Original content, clear navigation, editorial policy, corrections, privacy, about, and contact routes remain part of the approval-ready site structure.
